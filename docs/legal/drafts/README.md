# Legal document drafts (not published)

Working drafts of the EMBR Privacy Policy and Terms of Use, moved off the
public website on 2026-10-02 because EMBR is not yet incorporated and the
documents still contain placeholders (legal entity, registered address,
governing law, effective date). They are the starting point for counsel once
the entity and data architecture are final.

Public sign-up is paused until then (see `PUBLIC_REGISTRATION_ENABLED` in
`docs/DEPLOYMENT.md`). The app's consent screens still reference
`LEGAL_DOCUMENT_URLS` in `packages/validation`; those pages must be live and
final before sign-up reopens.
