Your health record

This is the information you choose to enter. It is health information, which the law treats as especially sensitive.

4. How patterns and the EMBR BRIEF work

Patterns in EMBR, such as how often a symptom appears or which symptoms were logged on the same days, are calculated by fixed, transparent rules from the entries you make. They describe your data. They are not a diagnosis and do not identify causes.

When you generate an EMBR BRIEF, we send a summary to Anthropic, our AI service provider, so it can help phrase the brief in plain language. That summary contains only:

  • the date range you chose;
  • counts of each symptom category and their severity;
  • a cycle summary (average cycle length and counts); and
  • the patterns our rules already found, with treatments described only generically (for example, “a treatment”).

It never includes your notes, treatment names, name, email address or account identifiers. The AI service is instructed not to diagnose, suggest causes or recommend treatment, and its output is checked against those rules before it reaches you. 5. Employers and organizations

Some organizations offer EMBR to their people. If you join through an organization:

  • Organization administrators can see that you are a member (your email address and role).
  • They can see only aggregated, anonymized trends across members. If too few people are included to protect individuals (currently fewer than 5), nothing is shown.
  • They can never see your individual symptom entries, cycle entries, notes, treatments or briefs.

9. Your rights and choices

Many of these you can do yourself in the app:

  • Access and portability: export your symptom entries, cycle entries and treatments as CSV, or a clinician summary as PDF, from the Export page.
  • Correction: edit or delete individual entries.
  • Deletion: delete your account from Settings. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time, and deleting your account ends our processing of your health record.
  • Sessions: review your signed-in devices and sign them out from Settings.

You also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, email info@embrhealthcare.com. You can complain to a supervisory authority: in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk); in the EU, your local data protection authority; in Japan, the Personal Information Protection Commission.
