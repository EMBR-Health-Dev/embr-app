# EMBR Brand Foundation

Internal. Not for publication. The reference for anyone writing, designing
or building for EMBR: founders, engineers, clinical advisors, designers
and agencies.

## Positioning

> EMBR is evidence infrastructure for longitudinal women's health records.

**One line:** EMBR is building a better record of women's health over time.

**Category:** longitudinal health record; health information infrastructure.

**Not:** a wellness app, a diagnostic tool, an AI health coach or a care
provider.

**Core promise:** a clearer record of your experience over time.

## The conceptual engine

```
Experience → longitudinal record → observable patterns → better conversation
```

At company level: **Evidence → Record → Patterns → Conversation.**

Never _symptoms → AI → diagnosis_, and never _wellness → tracking →
recommendations_. Patterns are calculated by fixed rules; AI only puts an
already calculated result into words (see `embr-clinical-logic`).

**The maxim:** _EMBR describes. Your clinician interprets._

## Site architecture

| Page      | Job                        |
| --------- | -------------------------- |
| Founder   | Why this exists            |
| Meet Maya | How it works               |
| Evidence  | Why to trust it            |
| Resources | What you can learn from it |

The Founder page is reached from About, not the main navigation. EMBR is
not a founder-led personal brand: the founder identified a systems problem
and is building the infrastructure around it. She does not claim to be
the clinical authority.

Maya is fictional and her record is synthetic, and every page that shows
her says so. Every figure about Maya is calculated from her record, never
typed in.

Evidence keeps four things apart: **what is established**, **what is
emerging**, **what EMBR does**, **what EMBR does not do**. Restraint is a
credibility signal.

## Principles

**Evidence.** Every factual or medical claim has a source, or it is removed.
No invented statistics, sources, testimonials or partners.

**Clarity.** Say what the record shows and stop there.

**Continuity.** Patterns over time, not snapshots.

**Agency.** The record belongs to the person. Export, share or delete it
when they choose.

**Restraint.** Say what EMBR does not do as clearly as what it does.

## Voice

Calm. Precise. Human. Editorial. Clinically respectful.

1. **Intelligent**, not simplistic.
2. **Calm**, never alarmist.
3. **Precise:** every claim has a boundary.
4. **Human**, but not sentimental.
5. **Quietly ambitious:** infrastructure, not another wellness subscription.

Sounds like:

- A clearer record of your experience.
- Patterns over time, not snapshots.
- Information to bring into the conversation.
- Your experience, recorded over time.
- Evidence where it exists. Clarity where it helps.

Does not sound like:

- "Take control of your hormones."
- "Finally understand your symptoms."
- "AI powered women's health."
- "Your body is trying to tell you something."

## Language

| Use          | Avoid                                         |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| record       | fix, cure                                     |
| experience   | predict, forecast                             |
| patterns     | diagnose, detect (a condition)                |
| history      | optimise hormones                             |
| evidence     | AI doctor, AI insights                        |
| conversation | personalised treatment                        |
| care         | causal wording ("X causes Y", "because of X") |

**Vocabulary hierarchy.** "Evidence" belongs to the company level (how
EMBR is built, what research shows). In the product the person keeps a
**record**: "your record", "recorded days", "observed patterns". A pattern
in a record is described as such: _"This is a pattern in Maya's record,
not a medical conclusion."_

**Blank days.** A day with no entry is "no entry recorded", never
"no symptoms".

## Writing rules

- No em dashes or hyphens in brand copy. Rewrite the sentence instead
  ("check in", "evidence based", "long term").
- Bold only the words that carry the meaning.
- No HIPAA claims, no published pricing, no pre-checked boxes.
- Questions a person may bring to care are framed as questions, never as
  conclusions.
- English and Japanese are written together; Japanese copy is reviewed by
  a native speaker before launch.

| -------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Plum     | Structure and primary actions         |
| Lilac    | Editorial accent, selection, severity |

| Graphite | Text                                  |

JP). Documents such as the EMBR BRIEF should look like documents, not
dashboards. Severity is readable without colour.
